Folio 1

A bank statement already contains its own proof.

Balance above + Credit − Debit = Balance
  • Proven as printed. The arithmetic holds.
  • Repaired, and the change is logged next to the row.
  • Left for you, with what it found.

Ithamar reads an Indian bank statement PDF, then checks every row against that one line of arithmetic. A row that holds is proven. A row that does not is explained, corrected only when the correction is earned, or handed to you.

Every row obeys one line of arithmetic.

The balance above, plus what came in, minus what went out, equals the balance printed. Ithamar walks that line down the page with a ruler, the way an auditor does. When it holds, the row is ticked as proven. Nothing is guessed.

Row 4 does not add up.

₹64.27 was read as money coming in. The balances say it went out. Move it to the debit column and this row closes, and so does the next. A missing debit of ₹128.54 would also balance it, but only by coincidence, so that alternative is logged and set aside.

One digit, read wrong.

The debit came out as 42,701.59. The balances on either side need 42,706.59: a single 1 that should be a 6. That is the kind of mistake text extraction actually makes, so it is the only kind of amount Ithamar will correct.

One bad cell, two breaks.

When a balance itself is misread, its row breaks and so does the row below, because the next row starts from the wrong figure. Ithamar recognises the pattern and corrects the one cell. Both rows close. The corrected row is marked repaired, not proven: those are different claims.

A row is missing. Ithamar will not invent it.

Between the 16th and the 18th, ₹1,76,090.10 arrived and no row records it. A missing row can explain any break, which is exactly why it proves nothing. The gap and its amount are handed to you.

Carried forward.

The closing balance matches. Nineteen rows are proven as printed, three are repaired with every change logged, and one is left for a person. A tool that quietly smooths that last row over is not auditing anything.

Zero rows marked proven that were wrong.

Across 24 generated statements, 855 rows and 39 injected errors, in three layouts modelled on Indian banks. Every swapped column, misread digit and misread balance was repaired to its true value. Every missing row was flagged and left alone.

These statements come from Ithamar's own generator, so their truth is known exactly. That proves the verifier. Real bank PDFs are the next test, and the result will be published the same way, failures included.

Why Ithamar

This is the sum of the tabernacle, even of the tabernacle of testimony, as it was counted, according to the commandment of Moses, for the service of the Levites, by the hand of Ithamar, son to Aaron the priest.

Exodus 38:21, King James Version

The first audit in the record was kept by Ithamar. Its silver can still be checked. Exodus 38 counts 603,550 men giving half a shekel each, and separately reports the total as 100 talents and 1,775 shekels. At 3,000 shekels to the talent, the two agree.

Run it on your machine

The same engine ships as a Python command. It exits 0 when every row is proven, 1 when rows were repaired, and 2 when a person needs to look, so it can guard a script.
